**What it is**

Breaking the Procrastination Pattern: Emotion-Regulation Edition. Part of the Procrastitype book series, one edition per procrastination type. It's not another scheduling app and it doesn't ask you to become unfeeling. It's a short, plain-spoken book that shows you that you're not avoiding the task, you're avoiding how it feels, and that waiting for the right mood is what keeps the mood broken.

**Why it works for Emotion-Regulation Procrastinators**

Every chapter targets your specific pattern: the boredom, anxiety, and overwhelm that feel like gates before you can work, and the relief that deepens the habit. It's a reading book, no fill-in-the-blanks and no homework. Read it in a few sittings and doing the work stops being something you need to feel ready for first.
