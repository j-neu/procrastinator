# Breaking the Procrastination Pattern (Perfectionist Edition) - Structure

**Target Word Count:** 15,000 - 20,000 words
**Tone:** Informal, authoritative, empathetic, direct. No references to Allen Carr.
**Core Philosophy:** The Perfectionist Procrastinator isn't "lazy" or "careless"; they are addicted to the *relief of not having to face an imperfect result*. They believe the lie that "I can only start when I'm certain it will be perfect." The book dismantles this belief, showing that perfection is a moving target that never arrives, and that the standards are a cage, not a crown.
