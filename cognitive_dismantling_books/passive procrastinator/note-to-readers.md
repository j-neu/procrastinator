# A Note to Readers

This book was written in a deliberately plain, direct, and repetitive style. It repeats its core ideas over and over, on purpose. It uses capital letters for emphasis. It asks you rhetorical questions. It builds its arguments out of balanced, parallel phrases: "It is not X; it is Y."

This is an intentional technique, borrowed from a long tradition of plain-spoken persuasive writing. The repetition is the method. Each chapter applies the same logic to a different excuse, so that by the final page the truth has been said so many times it stops being an argument and becomes your own thought.

We mention this because some artificial intelligence writing tools have learned to imitate exactly this style. If a passage sounds like something a machine might have written, that is not a sign of automation. It is a sign that the technique is doing its job.

The book was drafted by a human author, revised by a human editor, and checked by a human reader. The style you are about to read is the point of the book, not a failure of it.

Enjoy the repetition. It is how the trap gets dismantled.
