**What it is**

Breaking the Procrastination Pattern: Active Edition. Part of the Procrastitype book series, one edition per procrastination type. It's not a list of productivity tips and it doesn't ask you to slow down. It's a short, plain-spoken book that shows you that strategic delay is a tolerance you've built to adrenaline, not a strategy, and that the pressure was never the fuel, it was the tax.

**Why it works for Active Procrastinators**

Every chapter targets your specific pattern: the last-minute sprint, the crash after the all-nighter, the identity built on deadline heroics. It's a reading book, no fill-in-the-blanks and no homework. Read it in a few sittings and the delay stops feeling like a superpower and starts looking like the compulsion it always was.
