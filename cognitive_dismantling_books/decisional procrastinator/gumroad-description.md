**What it is**

Breaking the Procrastination Pattern: Decisional Edition. Part of the Procrastitype book series, one edition per procrastination type. It's not a decision-making framework and it won't tell you to stop thinking. It's a short, plain-spoken book that shows you that the search for the perfect choice was never carefulness. It's fear dressed up as diligence, and delay makes the choice for you by default.

**Why it works for Decisional Procrastinators**

Every chapter targets your specific pattern: the endless comparing, the waiting for certainty, the fear of the wrong choice. It's a reading book, no fill-in-the-blanks and no homework. Read it in a few sittings and choosing becomes something you can do, not something you wait to feel ready for.
