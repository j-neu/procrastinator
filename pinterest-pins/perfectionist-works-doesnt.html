“the editor” starts everything, ships nothing

WHAT WORKS.
WHAT DOESN'T.

Anything less than flawless feels like proof you're not good enough. So not starting feels safer than starting badly. Here's what actually gets you shipping.
✓ WHAT WORKS
Make the first draft deliberately bad. A draft is material, not a verdict. You can't edit a blank page.
Set the "good enough" bar first. Write down what done looks like before you start. Meet it. Stop.
Timebox the polish. One hour, not one week. Perfection has no finish line unless you give it one.
✗ WHAT DOESN'T
Waiting until it "feels ready." That feeling only shows up after the work is done. The wait never ends.
Chasing flawless on the first pass. Perfection eats unlimited time because it has no finish line.
Avoiding feedback. It just leaves the fear unchallenged and the project unfinished.
