psychology says…

You're not lazy.
You're one of 7 types of procrastinator.

(and knowing which one changes everything)
7
Researchers (Ferrari, Chu & Choi) have mapped 7 distinct patterns behind why you delay. Find yours below.
1
Arousal ("the thrill seeker")
Waits for the adrenaline. Swears the pressure makes the work better.
MOST COMMON · 54%
2
Avoidant ("the shield")
Delays to dodge the fear: of failing, of judgment, even of succeeding.
3
Decisional ("the freezer")
Won't move until the "right" choice appears. So nothing moves.
4
Perfectionist ("the editor")
Starts fine. Never ships. It's never quite good enough yet.
5
Passive ("the drifter")
Loses track of time until the deadline is already gone.
6
Active ("the sprinter")
Delays on purpose, then runs the pressure like a tool.
7
Emotion-Reg. ("the escaper")
Delays to feel better right now. Pays for it later.
