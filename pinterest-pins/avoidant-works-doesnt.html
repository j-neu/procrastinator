you're an avoidant procrastinator
“the shield” protects you from fear, not from deadlines

WHAT WORKS.
WHAT DOESN'T.

You're not avoiding the task. You're avoiding the feeling it triggers: fear of failing, of judgment, even of succeeding. Here's what actually breaks the loop.
✓ WHAT WORKS
Name the exact fear. Not "this project." The real one, out loud: "I'm scared my boss will think I'm incompetent."
Do a microscopic version. Open the document. If even that's scary, just sit in the chair.
Schedule the discomfort. Same 20 minutes, same time, every day, whether you want to or not.
✗ WHAT DOESN'T
"Just do it." Forcing through unnamed fear just proves to your brain the task really was dangerous.
Waiting to feel confident first. Confidence arrives after you act, never before.
Waiting for the fear to pass. It doesn't shrink while you avoid it. It grows.
